Investors

We don't take investment. Not now, not later — and it's worth saying why, because the reason is the whole point of this place.

When you take investors, you sell off control. Every raise is a trade: their money for a say in what the company becomes. We're not interested in that trade. We'd rather build slower and answer to no one than build faster and answer to a board.

We can say that because we're in a lucky spot. Most companies raise money because they have to — they need capital before they have anything worth selling. We skipped that part. We came to the table with a deep, well-built body of monetizable IP, made without outside money, using Loop MMT. The thing that usually requires a war chest, we made by hand.

So we're happy bootstrapping our way through the world. It's slower. It's ours. And it means we get to call every shot. That's not a limitation we're apologizing for — it's the arrangement we chose on purpose.
