Fact sheet

The Department of FWWC, Inc. — a small store, incorporated in Delaware, operating from New Gloucester, Maine. It sells a few things its makers made: digital goods (prompts, protocols, documents) and physical ones.

The store is static-first, keepless, and marketing-free. It holds no card data (a payment processor does; the store keeps only a reference), keeps no account for you, and refuses — by construction, not policy — fake urgency, scarcity theater, pre-ticked boxes, and roach-motel cancellations. Every order is an append-only, hash-linked receipt the buyer can verify.

It was built on a $200 laptop in a one-room RV in Maine. The best of what its makers build is given away free, MIT-licensed, with no account and no catch. The commerce spine and the free tools are open to read — the posture is not asked to be taken on faith.
